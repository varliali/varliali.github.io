Traffic Rush – Car Racing

The classic traffic racer is back, rebuilt in 3D! Weave through busy highway traffic at full speed, fill your nitro with near misses and race against the clock across five worlds.

Steer with a touch, tilt your phone or use buttons. Pass cars by a hair for near-miss points and combos up to ×5 – every near miss fills your nitro. Dodge oil slicks, mud and cones, grab coins, fuel cans and nitro bottles, and reach each checkpoint before the clock runs out. Speed past a patrol car and the police give chase: shake them off with nitro for a bonus.

60 RACES IN 5 WORLDS

  • Deadwood — Desert highways under the burning sun
  • Alpine Valley — Mountain roads through green valleys
  • Istanbul — Two-way roads with oncoming traffic, in the evening light
  • Snowy Pass — Snow, fog and icy mountain passes
  • Neon City — The city at night, glowing in the rain

Beat the clock, rack up near misses, hold your top speed or reach the finish in one piece – and at the end of every world, race a rival to the line.

FEATURES

  • Garage — 8 cars, from a nimble rally car to a formula racer; upgrade top speed, acceleration, brakes and handling, and paint them in your favourite colours
  • Endless Mode — Drive as far as you can: every checkpoint refuels you, but the pace keeps rising
  • Leaderboards — Weekly and all-time rankings; add friends with a code and beat their scores
  • Daily Fun — Daily missions, a daily reward that grows every day, a free gift box every few hours and double coins for your first win of the day
  • Play Anywhere — Races play offline, no account needed; km/h or mph
  • Multiple Languages — Available in English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Indonesian
  • Premium — One purchase removes the ads between races, gives you video rewards without watching videos, unlocks the Pursuit police supercar and adds 3,000 coins

Buckle up, hit the nitro and race!
