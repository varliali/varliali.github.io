Soccer Crash

Smash the forts, rescue your team! Soccer Crash is a physics puzzle game where you kick balls at the forts of the Jester Gang and bring them crashing down.

It's the final day and the stadium is packed, when the Jester Gang crashes the party, sets the stands on fire and kidnaps your teammates. Drag the ball to aim, release to kick, and watch wood splinter, glass shatter and stone crumble. Knock out every hooligan to clear the level, and find the smartest shot to earn all three stars.

FIVE BALLS, FIVE TRICKS

  • Football — The trusty classic. No tricks, just a solid kick.
  • Tennis Ball — Tap while it flies to split it in two.
  • Cricket Ball — Sets wooden blocks on fire, and the flames spread to the wood they touch.
  • Basketball — Big and heavy: knocks down tall towers.
  • Bowling Ball — Explodes a second after it hits, or when you tap.

FEATURES

  • 135 Levels — Three worlds of 45 levels each: the sunny Stadium, the Snowy Mountains and the Night Match
  • Boss Fights — Every world ends with a tough boss and your caged teammates waiting to be freed. Can you topple the Jester King?
  • A Story to Follow — Short comic scenes tell how the gang struck, and how the Captain wins the day
  • Real Physics — Every block can tumble, roll and break; no two shots play out the same
  • Daily Rewards — Come back every day for growing rewards, and open a free gift box every few hours
  • Pick Your Balls — Spend your coins on the balls each fort needs, with new balls and extra slots unlocked as you progress
  • Three Stars — Break more blocks and save balls for a higher score, and the game tells you how many points the next star needs
  • Fresh, Bright Look — Redrawn in HD with lively stadiums, cheering crowds, flying debris and gentle haptics
  • Play Anywhere — No account and no internet connection needed to play
  • Multiple Languages — Available in English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Indonesian
  • Premium — One purchase removes the ads between levels, gives you video rewards without watching videos, and adds 5,000 coins

Quick to learn and hard to put down: one more kick, one more fort, one more star.
