Aliens: The Puzzle

Bring the tribes home! Aliens: The Puzzle is a cosmic match-3 game: swipe an alien to swap it with its neighbour, line up three of the same tribe, and they beam aboard the mothership.

Once every hundred years, the nine tribes of the Glow Belt gather aboard the mothership Halo, and together their glow lights a brand-new star. But this year the rogue comet Krogg came crashing through. His icy tail froze the planets and scattered the tribes across the galaxy. Help Blu, the youngest of the Halo's crew and the festival's lamplighter, travel from world to world, break Krogg's ice, crack his meteors and bring everyone home before the Festival of Lights.

JOIN THEIR GLOW

  • Pulse — Four in a row: clears everything around it.
  • Cross — An L or T shape: clears a whole row and column.
  • Nova — Five in a row: swap it with any alien to beam up its whole tribe.
  • Combos — Swap two specials together for a bigger blast. Two Novas clear the whole board!
