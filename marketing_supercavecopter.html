Super Cave Copter

One-Tap Helicopter Arcade

The classic cave copter is back! Tap to lift your helicopter through an endless, winding cave, dodge the rocks and keep your fuel tank from running dry.

Every tap gives your rotor a lift; stop tapping and gravity pulls you down. The cave never ends, but it gets faster and narrower the deeper you fly. Rocks hang in your way, and past 1,500 points they start to move. Watch your fuel gauge: grab the red fuel cans before the engine sputters out, or you'll drop like a stone.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap — Each tap lifts the helicopter; stop tapping to let it sink
  • Avoid — The cave walls and the rocks in your path
  • Refuel — Collect fuel cans to keep the rotor spinning
  • Go deeper — Every 500 points the cave changes color, and the challenge keeps growing

FEATURES

  • Simple to Learn — One-touch controls anyone can pick up in seconds
  • Hard to Master — Rising speed, a narrowing cave and moving rocks keep every run exciting
  • Retro Pixel Art — Classic pixel graphics with an 8-bit soundtrack
  • Daily Cave — A new cave every day, the same for everyone; climb today's ranking
  • Hangar — Beat your best to unlock new helicopter colors, all the way to gold
  • Leaderboards — Daily, weekly and all-time rankings; pick your pilot name and see who is just ahead of you
  • Ad-Free Option — A one-time purchase removes the ads and lets you keep flying without a video
  • Play Anywhere — No account needed; play offline, your scores are sent when you are back online
  • Multiple Languages — Available in English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Indonesian
