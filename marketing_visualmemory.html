Visual Memory

Look, remember, tap! Visual Memory is a calm, modern brain trainer that sharpens your short-term and visual memory in just a few minutes a day.

A few tiles light up on the board for a moment, then go dark. Tap the ones that were lit. Get it right and the board grows with one more tile to remember; slip up and it steps back. How many tiles can you hold in your mind?

THREE GAME MODES

  • Classic — Remember which tiles light up. Fifteen tries to climb as high as you can, from 3 tiles up to 16.
  • Sequence — The tiles are numbered: tap them back in the right order. The ultimate test of your visual memory.
  • Timed — Two minutes on the clock. Study each pattern as long as you like, then reproduce as many as you can before time runs out.

FEATURES

  • Daily Challenge — A new set of ten patterns every day, the same for every player. Earn up to three stars and see the countdown to tomorrow's challenge.
  • Streaks — Play every day to build your streak. Missed a day? You can still save it.
  • Gentle Reminders — Optional evening reminder on days you haven't played yet
  • Statistics — Best scores, accuracy, a calendar of your daily challenges, and a chart of your progress over your last games
  • Show Again — Lost the pattern? See it one more time
  • Clean, Modern Design — Calm colours, smooth animations and gentle haptics, in light and dark themes
  • Play Anywhere — No account and no internet connection needed to play
  • Multiple Languages — Available in English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Indonesian
  • Premium — One purchase removes the ads between games and makes Continue, Show Again and saving your streak free

Suitable for everyone, from students to seniors: a few minutes of practice a day is all it takes to notice the difference.
