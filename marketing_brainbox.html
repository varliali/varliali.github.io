Brain Box Puzzle

Push, think, and solve! Brain Box Puzzle is a classic box-pushing brain teaser that is easy to learn and hard to put down.

Your goal is simple: push every box onto its target. But every move counts — a box pushed into the wrong corner can't be pulled back out. Plan ahead, find the clever path, and solve each warehouse in as few moves as you can.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Only one box can be pushed at a time.
  • A box cannot be pulled.
  • You cannot walk through boxes or walls.
  • The puzzle is solved when every box sits on a target.

FEATURES

  • 276 Hand-Picked Levels — 240 puzzles in 20 packs, rising from Beginner to Expert, plus the 36 levels of the original Brain Box in Classic mode
  • Easy Controls — Tap where you want to go, tap a box next to you to push it, or simply swipe
  • Unlimited Undo & Redo — Made a mistake? Step back as far as you like and try a different idea
  • Hints — Stuck on a tricky level? A hint shows you the next correct push
  • Earn Stars — Solve a level in the fewest moves to collect all three stars, and try to beat your own best
  • Stuck Box Warning — The game tells you when a box can no longer reach a target, so you never waste time on a lost position
  • Auto-Save — Leave a level any time and continue right where you left off
  • Light & Dark Themes — Pick the look you like, with relaxing background music you can switch on or off
  • Play Anywhere — No account and no internet connection needed to play
  • Multiple Languages — Available in English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Indonesian
  • Premium — One purchase unlocks every level, removes the ads between levels, and gives unlimited hints

From gentle warm-ups to real head-scratchers, every level is a small puzzle waiting for that satisfying "aha!" moment. How many can you solve?
