Deep Diver

Plunge into the ocean depths in Deep Diver — a fast-paced underwater adventure where every second and every breath counts!

Guide your diver as deep as possible, dodging hazards, blasting enemies, and grabbing coins along the way. Watch your oxygen meter — when it hits zero, your dive is over. How deep can you go before you run out of air?

FEATURES

  • Endless Diving — Swim as deep as you can in this addictive one-more-try adventure
  • Simple Controls — Play with touch, or switch to tilt controls in Settings
  • Power-Ups — Grab shields, blasters, speed boosts, and jetpacks to survive longer and dive deeper
  • Upgrades — Spend your coins to permanently boost swim speed, ammo, shield capacity, oxygen tank size, and jetpack duration
  • Daily Quests — Complete fresh challenges every day for bonus coins
  • Global Leaderboard — Compete with divers around the world for the deepest dive
  • Cloud Save — Sign in to keep your progress safe across devices — completely optional, play anonymously if you prefer
  • Multiple Languages — Available in English, Turkish, German, Spanish, Portuguese, French, and Indonesian

Every dive is different. Every second brings you closer to a new record. Take a breath, dive in, and see how deep you can go!
