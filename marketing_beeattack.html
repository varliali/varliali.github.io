Bee Attack: Honey Cut

Cut the honeycomb, dodge the bees! Bee Attack is a quick arcade puzzle game: draw a rectangle on the honeycomb, and a saw cuts it out – as long as no angry bee touches the line.

Grandma's birthday is tomorrow, and Bubu the bear cub wants to bake her the best honey cake ever. But the pantry is empty, and bees don't like to share. Drag your finger to draw a rectangle, let go, and a saw runs round it. When it gets back to the start, the piece falls out. If a bee touches the red line while the saw is running, the cut breaks and you lose a life. Cut 70% of the honeycomb to clear the level, and more for three stars.

SIX KINDS OF ANGRY BEES

  • Normal Bee — Flies on the diagonal, just like in the original game.
  • Fast Bee — Quick, and flies at any angle.
  • Flash Bee — Darts, then hovers. Watch the rhythm!
  • Fat Bee — Slow but big: hard to get around.
  • Soldier Bee — Chases your saw while it cuts.
  • Queen Bee — Waits at the end of every world, with a few tricks of her own.
